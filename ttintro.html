Teen Titans (TV series)

Teen Titans is an American animated superhero television series developed by Glen Murakami and David Slack. It is based on the DC Comics characters of the same name.

The Main Team

  • Robin - Leader.
  • Starfire - An alien princess from Tamaran.
  • Cyborg - Half-human and Half-robot.
  • Raven - A half-demon and Half-human magic-user.
  • Beast Boy - A shapeshifter who turns into animals.

Villains and Rivals

  1. Primary Antagonists
    1. Slade (Deathstroke)
    2. Trigon
  2. The H.I.V.E. Academy
