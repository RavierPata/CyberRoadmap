Why Certifications Matter

Getting certified is a great way to prove you actually know your stuff. It helps your resume stand out to recruiters and usually leads to a much better starting salary. If you're just starting out, CompTIA is the go-to, while advanced certs like CISSP or CISM are what you'll aim for once you've got a few years of experience
