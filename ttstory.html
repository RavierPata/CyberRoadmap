The Origins of the Teen Titans

Every member of the team comes from a unique background. Before they moved into the tower, each had their own stories and pasts.

Member Original Home Backstory Snippet
Robin Gotham City The former protege of Batman who moved to Jump City to lead his own team.
Starfire Tamaran An alien princess who escaped her duties as a princess to find a home on Earth.
Cyborg Detroit A star athlete rebuilt with cybernetic parts after a tragic accident.
Raven Azarath The daughter of the demon Trigon who had escaped from his grasp.
Beast Boy Upper Lamumba After contracting a rare illness, he was given a serum that turned him green and gave him animal powers.
